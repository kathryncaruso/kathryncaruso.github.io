_Using Difco™ Brain Heart Infusion Broth_

## Overview

Brain Heart Infusion (BHI) broth is a general-purpose, nutrient-rich liquid medium. Used in this project for growing *Sporosarcina pasteurii* starter cultures as a positive control in ureolytic activity assays.

---

## Materials
- Difco™ Brain Heart Infusion (BHI) powder
- Purified water
- Autoclave-safe bottle (use ≤50% capacity)
- 50 mL Falcon tubes (for dispensing)

---

## Final Concentrations

| Component            | Final Concentration |
| -------------------- | ------------------- |
| Brain Heart Infusion | 37 g/L              |

---

## Procedure

1. Suspend **37 g** of BHI powder in **1 L** of distilled water.
    - **Important:** Fill bottle to no more than 50% capacity.
2. **Heat to dissolve.**
3. Cool, mix well, and distribute into final containers.
4. Autoclave at **121°C for 15 minutes**.

---

## Batch Variations

| Batch Size | BHI Powder | Purified Water | Notes |
| ---------- | ---------- | -------------- | ----- |
| Small | 1.85 g | 50 mL | ~5 Falcon tubes at 10 mL each |
| Medium | 3.7 g | 100 mL | ~10 Falcon tubes at 10 mL each |
| Large | 9.25 g | 250 mL | ~25 Falcon tubes at 10 mL each |
| Full | 37 g | 1 L | ~100 Falcon tubes at 10 mL each |

---

## Dispensing

1. In a Biosafety Cabinet, aseptically dispense **10 mL** of sterile BHI broth per **50 mL Falcon tube**.
2. Cap tightly and label with medium type and date.

---

## Storage

- Store sterile broth at **4°C**.
- Label with medium type, date of preparation, and initials.
- Stable for several weeks refrigerated. Discard if turbidity develops.

---

## Notes

- For *S. pasteurii* starter cultures, inoculate a single Falcon tube (10 mL) from a freezer stock using a sterile loop — see [S. pasteurii Starter Culture SOP](/methods/s-pasteurii-starter/).
- BHI broth does **not** contain agar. For BHI agar plates, see [BHI Agar SOP](/methods/bhi-agar/).
