# Succinate-Urea Growth Medium

*Standard Operating Procedure*

<div style="border-left: 4px solid #2d6a4f; background: #f0fdf4; padding: 12px; margin: 12px 0;">
<span style="color: #2d6a4f; font-weight: bold;">Purpose:</span> Preparation of a succinate-based growth medium supplemented with 2% urea (20 g/L) for culturing cold-adapted bacterial isolates in ureolytic activity assays. Succinate serves as the sole carbon source, with concentration matched on a molar carbon basis to a 0.5 g/L glucose reference (16.67 mmol C/L). The medium can be used for growth experiments, ureolytic activity assays, or any application requiring a defined carbon source with urea.
</div>

---

## 1 Equipment and materials

### 1.1 Equipment

- Analytical balance
- Magnetic stir bar and stir plate
- Volumetric flasks or graduated cylinders
- pH meter or pH strips
- Autoclave
- Biosafety cabinet
- 0.22 µm syringe filters
- Sterile syringes

### 1.2 Chemicals

- Sodium succinate dibasic hexahydrate (MW 270.14 g/mol; Sigma-Aldrich Cat. No. S2378)
- Yeast extract
- K<sub>2</sub>HPO<sub>4</sub> (potassium phosphate dibasic)
- Urea
- 1 M HCl (for pH adjustment)
- 1 M NaOH (for pH adjustment)
- Milli-Q water

---

## 2 Base medium formulation

**Per liter:**

| Component | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yeast extract | 0.1 g |  |
| Sodium succinate dibasic hexahydrate | 1.126 g | MW 270.14 g/mol; provides 16.67 mmol C/L |
| K<sub>2</sub>HPO<sub>4</sub> | 0.3 g | Potassium phosphate dibasic |
| Milli-Q water | to 1000 mL |  |
| pH target | 6.8 ± 0.2 | Adjust with 1 M HCl or NaOH |

<div style="border-left: 4px solid #2d6a4f; background: #f0fdf4; padding: 12px; margin: 12px 0;">
<span style="color: #2d6a4f; font-weight: bold;">Note — Carbon equivalence calculation:</span> Glucose reference: 0.5 g/L ÷ 180.16 g/mol = 2.776 mmol/L × 6 C = 16.67 mmol C/L. Succinate: 16.67 mmol C/L ÷ 4 C/molecule = 4.167 mmol/L × 270.14 g/mol = <strong>1.126 g/L</strong>. Note: hexahydrate form means a large fraction of the mass is water of crystallization.
</div>

---

## 3 Base medium preparation

1. Weigh components for the target volume (see [Section 5](#section-5-volume-planning-for-jung-assay-serial-transfer-experiment) for volume planning)
2. Dissolve in ~80% of target Milli-Q water volume using a magnetic stir bar and stir plate
3. Adjust pH to **6.8 ± 0.2** with 1 M HCl or 1 M NaOH
4. Bring to final volume with Milli-Q water
5. Autoclave on **liquid cycle** (slow exhaust) at 121°C, 15 psi — time depends on volume (see [Autoclave SOP](/methods/autoclave/)):
   - ≤ 1 L: **20 min**
   - 1 – 2 L: **40 min**
6. Cool to room temperature before adding urea stock (Section 4)

---

## 4 Urea supplementation

Urea is added separately via a filter-sterilized stock to a final concentration of 2% (20 g/L).

### 4.1 10% (w/v) urea stock preparation

<div style="border-left: 4px solid #c0713a; background: #fffbf0; padding: 12px; margin: 12px 0;">
<span style="color: #c0713a; font-weight: bold;">Important:</span> Milli-Q water must be <strong>autoclaved in advance</strong> before use in urea stock preparation. Autoclave at 121°C, 15 psi, 15–20 min (liquid cycle) and cool to room temperature before dissolving urea. See [Autoclave Sterilization SOP](/methods/autoclave/).
</div>

1. Weigh 20 g urea per 200 mL autoclaved Milli-Q water (10% w/v)
2. Dissolve at room temperature (do **not** heat — urea degrades at high temperature)
3. Filter-sterilize through 0.22 µm syringe filter in biosafety cabinet
4. Aliquot into pre-sterilized containers (~50 mL per aliquot for weekly use)
5. Store at 4°C

<div style="border-left: 4px solid #2d6a4f; background: #f0fdf4; padding: 12px; margin: 12px 0;">
<span style="color: #2d6a4f; font-weight: bold;">Note — Urea stability:</span> Urea in aqueous solution is chemically stable at room temperature and pH 4–8 for months (non-enzymatic hydrolysis is negligible). The primary concern is <strong>microbial contamination</strong>, not chemical degradation. Batch preparation with aseptic aliquoting is preferred over weekly re-preparation.
</div>

### 4.2 Combining base medium and urea stock

- Work in biosafety cabinet
- C<sub>1</sub>V<sub>1</sub> = C<sub>2</sub>V<sub>2</sub>: 10% × V<sub>1</sub> = 2% × final volume
- **V<sub>1</sub> = 20% of final volume** as urea stock
- **Remaining 80%** = autoclaved base medium
- Mix gently by swirling

## 5 Volume planning (for Jung assay serial transfer experiment)

**Per transfer window:**

| Use | Volume per tube | Number of tubes | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isolate cultures (20 × 3 replicates) | 3 mL | 60 | 180 mL |
| Positive control (*S. pasteurii* × 3) | 3 mL | 3 | 9 mL |
| Negative control (uninoculated × 3) | 3 mL | 3 | 9 mL |
| **Subtotal per transfer** |  | **66** | **198 mL** |
| Overage (~15%) |  |  | ~30 mL |
| **Prepare per transfer** |  |  | **~230 mL** |

**For entire 28-day experiment (4 transfers):** ~920 mL (~1 L) total medium

### 5.1 Urea stock and base medium volumes per transfer

| Component | Volume per transfer | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10% urea stock | **50 mL** | Provides comfortable overage beyond the ~40 mL minimum |
| Autoclaved base medium | **200 mL** | 80% of 250 mL total |
| **Total complete medium** | **250 mL** | ~52 mL overage beyond the 198 mL needed |

### 5.2 Batch urea stock preparation (recommended)

For a 4-transfer experiment, prepare the full stock on Day −3 and aliquot:

| Item | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urea | 20 g | 10% w/v |
| Autoclaved Milli-Q water | 200 mL | Cooled to RT |
| Total stock | 200 mL | Filter-sterilize through 0.22 µm |
| Aliquot into | 4 × 50 mL | Pre-sterilized containers, labeled by transfer # |
| Storage | 4°C | Use one aliquot per weekly transfer |
